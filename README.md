## Driver's Edge: Data-Driven Fuel Savings for NYC Rideshare Drivers ($45,000+ and Counting)

This project investigates **fuel efficiency and vehicle selection** for NYC rideshare drivers, addressing the high and variable costs of fuel in urban transportation. By analyzing driver-collected data and applying machine learning, it identifies the most cost-effective vehicle choices, projects **over $45,000 in potential fuel savings over four years**, and highlights the broader economic and environmental benefits of optimized vehicle usage. The work was informed by research into average rideshare operating costs, vehicle fuel efficiency trends, and the growing adoption of hybrid and electric vehicles in city fleets.
